# Project Ideation - Odalis Torrres

## Idea 1: AI Brand Identity Generator for Small Businesses

**Problem/Goal:**
Small local businesses often can't afford a graphic designer. This project uses AI tools to generate a complete brand kit — logo concepts, color palettes, font pairings, and taglines — for a hypothetical or real small business.

**AI Tool(s):**
Adobe Firefly, Canva AI, ChatGPT, DALL·E

**Why This Interests Me:**
I want to combine my interest in graphic design with AI tools to create something useful and portfolio-worthy.

**Rough Scope:**
2-3 weeks

---

## Idea 2: AI vs. Designer — A Visual Comparison Study

**Problem/Goal:**
There is a lot of debate about whether AI can replace graphic designers. This project creates the same design asset both manually and using AI tools, then documents and analyzes the differences in quality, time, and creativity.

**AI Tool(s):**
Canva AI, Adobe Firefly, DALL·E

**Why This Interests Me:**
This is directly relevant to my future in design — I want to understand where AI helps and where it falls short.

**Rough Scope:**
1-2 weeks

---

## Idea 3: AI-Powered Social Media Content Kit

**Problem/Goal:**
Content creators and small businesses struggle to maintain a consistent visual brand across social media. This project uses AI to generate a reusable template kit for Instagram or Facebook — banner, post templates, and story frames.

**AI Tool(s):**
Canva AI, ChatGPT, Adobe Firefly

**Why This Interests Me:**
It is practical, immediately usable, and shows how AI can support real design workflows.

**Rough Scope:**
1-2 weeks
