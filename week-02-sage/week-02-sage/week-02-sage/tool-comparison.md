## Test Results

### Prompt 1: Simple Explanation
- ChatGPT is very similar to Claude and a bit more complex in its wordings, but at the end, it says the same.  
- Claude was fast and organized and uses wording that is clear to understand, and I go with its answer.  
- Gemini is somewhat disorganized and answers using some referrals from website info and/or Wikipedia. It's understandable that not all the information there is completely true, so people can enter information they think is certain.

### Prompt 2: Creative Task (Haiku)
- Gemini has more sense in this one, ChatGPT was slower while Claude was the fastest one.

### Prompt 3: Information Retrieval
- ChatGPT goes to the point, doesn’t develop its explanation, and is really acting as what it is, a machine.  
- Claude is clean, fast, and very easy to read the information provided.  
- Gemini is still kind of disorganized in placing things where they belong and still uses references for the information given.  
- While both are ways to make computers perform tasks, they differ fundamentally in how they arrive at a result.

### Prompt 4: Problem-Solving
- Claude is very clear, organized, and easy to follow.  
- ChatGPT is similar, but it takes longer to load information.  
- As for Gemini, I just don’t like it; it hurts my eyes.

## Comparative Analysis
Claude was consistently the fastest and most organized tool, providing clear and easy-to-understand answers. ChatGPT is very similar to Claude in quality but is a bit slower at times. Gemini seemed less organized and relied on references that sometimes felt less reliable. Overall, I prefer Claude for quick and clear answers, but I use ChatGPT when I want more detail or creative responses.

## My Choice for Primary Tool
**Tool**: Claude  
**Reasoning**: It is clear, fast, and easy to understand, making it the best for most of my tasks.  
**Backup Plan**: Use ChatGPT when Claude is busy or if I need more detailed explanations.
